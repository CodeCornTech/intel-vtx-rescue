# Community conduct

Be respectful and specific. Review evidence and code without attacking the person who supplied them. Help newcomers provide reproducible, sanitized reports.

Do not publish private machine/customer data, credentials, harassment or unsolicited advertising. Do not pressure someone to restart an unattended machine without considering how they will regain access.

Maintainers may edit or remove content that violates these rules, explain the action where appropriate, and restrict participation after repeated violations. To report a concern involving private information, request a private reporting channel without repeating that information publicly.
